# Lahjio: what to publish, in what order (YouTube + Facebook)

Post at **8–10 pm Saudi time** (11 pm–1 am Bangladesh). **Bangla at 8 pm, English at 10 pm** on the same day.
Captions and hashtags are in each folder's notes: `POSTING-KIT.md`, `daily/POSTING-SCHEDULE.md`,
`coming-soon/POSTING.md`, `global-english/POSTING.md`.

## The order

| Day | Video | YouTube | Facebook page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Promo #1**: what Lahjio is (`final/`) | 16:9 as a normal video **and** 9:16 as a Short | 16:9 as a page video (**pin it to the top**) + 9:16 as a Reel |
| 2 | Daily day 1 · كم السعر | Short (9:16) | Reel (9:16) |
| 3 | Daily day 2 · وين الحمّام | Short | Reel |
| 4 | Daily day 3 · أبغى موية | Short | Reel |
| 5 | Daily day 4 · لِفّ يمين | Short | Reel |
| 6 | Daily day 5 · شوي شوي | Short | Reel |
| 7 | Daily day 6 · ما فهمت | Short | Reel |
| 8 | Daily day 7 · خلاص | Short | Reel |
| 9 | **Android app coming soon** (`coming-soon/`) | Short + 16:9 video | Reel + pin it (replace the promo pin) |
| 10 | **Learn Saudi Arabic in English** (`global-english/`, English only) | 16:9 video + 9:16 Short | 16:9 page video + Reel |

Why this order: the promo first tells new viewers what Lahjio is; the daily week builds a habit and followers;
the "coming soon" news lands best once people already follow you; the English-speaker video is for a new
audience, so it comes last and doesn't break the Bangla daily rhythm.

## Which file to upload

- **YouTube Short / Facebook Reel:** `TikTok-Reels-Shorts_9x16.mp4` (promo: `final/Lahjio-BN-9x16.mp4`)
- **YouTube normal video / Facebook page video:** `YouTube-Facebook_16x9.mp4` (promo: `final/Lahjio-BN-16x9.mp4`)
- Daily phrases: Shorts/Reels only. Don't also upload their 16:9 copy, it would be duplicate content.
  Later you can join the 7 days into one 16:9 "Week 1" video.
- Skip the `noMusic` files on YouTube and Facebook (they're for TikTok trending sounds). The 4:5 file is only needed
  if you post to the Facebook **feed** instead of Reels; Reels reach more people.

## YouTube setup (once)

- Playlists: "সৌদি আরবি – প্রতিদিন একটি বাক্য" (Bangla daily), "Saudi Arabic phrase of the day" (English daily),
  "Lahjio – about the app" (promo, coming soon, global).
- Every upload: put `lahjio.com` in the first line of the description, add it to the right playlist, Bengali videos
  set to language Bengali, English to English.
- Pin a comment with "ফ্রি শুরু করো 👉 lahjio.com".

## Facebook setup (once)

- Use the Page, not a personal profile. Add `lahjio.com` as the Page website and the "Sign up" button.
- Share each Bangla daily Reel into Bangladeshi-expat-in-Saudi groups (only where the group rules allow it).
- Reply to comments in the first hour; it pushes the Reel to more people.
