LAHJIO PROMO: VIDEO POSTING GUIDE
=================================
All videos are in this folder. Each is about 32 seconds long.
"noMusic" versions keep the sound effects and the Arabic voice but drop the music.

WHICH FILE GOES WHERE
---------------------
Instagram Reels / Stories ......... Lahjio-BN-9x16.mp4
TikTok ............................ Lahjio-BN-9x16-noMusic.mp4 (add a trending TikTok sound for more reach)
YouTube Shorts .................... Lahjio-BN-9x16.mp4
Facebook Reels .................... Lahjio-BN-9x16.mp4
YouTube video / Facebook feed ..... Lahjio-BN-16x9.mp4
English audience / ads ............ Lahjio-EN-9x16.mp4 and Lahjio-EN-16x9.mp4

Note: the Drive copies are compressed to about 5.5 MB to fit the upload limit. Social apps
re-compress uploads anyway. Full-quality originals are on the PC in lahjio-promo/out/final.
