## Rules
- **Start after the day 9 post.** Day 9 is white, so post 01 is green, then white, green, white… (odd numbers = green, even = white).
  Keeping this order keeps the green/white checkerboard on your profile grid. If you skip a post, also skip the next one (or swap two neighbours of the same colour), so the colours keep alternating.
- **One post a day** works best, about 8–10 pm Saudi time (11 pm–1 am Bangladesh time).
- **Carousel** = folder with `slide-1 … slide-5`. Upload all 5 together, in order. Only slide 1 shows on the grid.
- **Quiz posts:** the next day, pin a comment with the answer (answers are in the table).
- Reels: turn off "Show in profile grid" so the checkerboard stays clean.
- Hashtags for every post: `#সৌদিআরবি #আরবিশিখি #প্রবাসী #সৌদিপ্রবাসী #KSA #Riyadh #Jeddah #LearnArabic #SaudiArabic #Lahjio`
